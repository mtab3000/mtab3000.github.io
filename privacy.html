Privacy policy: rclone-bigboy

rclone-bigboy is a private Google OAuth client. Only its owner uses it, on the owner's own computer.

  • The app accesses Google Drive files only for the owner who signs in.
  • Files and OAuth tokens stay on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any other server.
  • The app does not collect, sell, or share data with third parties.
  • Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
